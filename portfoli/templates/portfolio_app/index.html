Farmer's Companion

AI-Powered Agricultural Assistant

Full-Stack Developer
June 2026

An AI-powered farming assistant built to reach Ugandan smallholder farmers who don't have smartphones or internet access — using USSD, SMS, and Voice on any basic 2G phone. Integrated Africa's Talking APIs and Sunflower AI (a Uganda-focused LLM) for localized agricultural support in Luganda, Runyankole, and Acholi.

Problem Solved

Rural Ugandan farmers lack access to timely agricultural advice, market prices, and weather data due to smartphone and connectivity barriers. This solution brings that information to any phone via USSD menus and SMS.

Key Engineering Highlights

  • ▸Built Django backend with SQLite for farmer profiles, crop data, and transaction history
  • ▸Used ngrok to test and validate webhooks in a sandbox environment
  • ▸Delivers real-time weather forecasts, market prices, pest-control advice, and farming tips
